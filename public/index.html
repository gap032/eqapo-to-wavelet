Three steps

          
How it works

        


        

          

            1
            
Find your EQ file

            

              Equalizer APO: C:\Program Files\EqualizerAPO\config\config.txt. Peace:
              peace.txt in the same folder.
            

          


          

            2
            
Upload and inspect

            

              Pick the device and sample rate. The chart shows the difference between the original EQ
              and what Wavelet will apply.
            

          


          

            3
            
Import into Wavelet

            

              In Wavelet open AutoEQ → GraphicEQ → Import
              and choose the downloaded file.
            

          

        

      


      

        
          
        
        

          
Your data stays on your phone

          

            The file is read and converted locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
